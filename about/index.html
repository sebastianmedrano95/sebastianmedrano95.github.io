Excellence is in the details that define every design.

Industrial designer & 3D artist · Melbourne, Australia

I bridge creativity, design, engineering, and technology to transform complex ideas into thoughtful, functional, and manufacturable products. My approach combines creative exploration, technical problem-solving, and a deep understanding of materials and manufacturing processes to develop practical and innovative design solutions. I enjoy challenging conventional approaches, exploring emerging technologies, and finding smarter, more efficient ways to bring ideas to life. Driven by curiosity and continuous learning, I strive to create designs that balance aesthetics, functionality, and real-world production requirements.

I'm an industrial designer with over 5 years of experience and a strong interest in design, technology, and how things work. Over the years, I've worked on more than 99 projects across different companies and industries, including furniture, advertising, education, retail, and commercial interiors.

My experience includes product development, 3D visualization, and design for manufacturing. I enjoy exploring new tools and technologies, experimenting with different approaches, and finding practical ways to solve design challenges. Outside of work, I've also developed four original product designs, giving me the freedom to explore my own ideas and interests.

Some of my personal projects have received recognition, including my 3D character Fishtume at The Rookies and first place in the professional category of the 2025 LEAP KeyShot Challenge for my project Mural – Spray Gun. My work has also been featured by Yanko Design and Trend Hunter.

I currently work as an industrial designer at BCI Furniture, developing custom furniture for commercial spaces. Over the past three years, I've worked on more than 70 production-ready designs, preparing technical drawings and documentation for manufacturing. I particularly enjoy the technical side of design, understanding how products are made, improving workflows, and finding more efficient ways to turn ideas into something real.
