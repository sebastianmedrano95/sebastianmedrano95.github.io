Pupseed — Grow Joy

2023

PupSeed is an interactive gardening toy that helps children plant and care for seeds. It combines remote control with a water tank, seed compartment, and an excavator attachment for preparing soil.

Moisture and proximity sensors monitor growing conditions, adjust watering and planting depth, and help the toy avoid obstacles. An expressive eye display communicates when it needs water or more seeds.
