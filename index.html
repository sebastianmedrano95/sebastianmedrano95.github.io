More about me ↗

Good design starts with a thoughtful question.

The objects around us should feel useful, considered, and made to last. I start by understanding the people, place, and practical needs behind a brief, then explore form, materials, and finishes with care. Each decision has to work visually and in use—and make sense to prototype, refine, and produce.

A few helpful details

Have any questions?

What kind of design work do you take on?

I work across custom furniture and joinery, product development, 3D visualization, and digital content. Depending on the brief, I can help shape an early concept, refine a product for manufacture, or create polished visuals that communicate the idea clearly.

How does your design process work?

We begin by discussing the brief, the people the product is for, the budget, and any production constraints. I then sketch and explore concepts, develop the agreed direction in CAD, and prepare the models, shop drawings, renders, or CNC files that the project requires.

How long does a project usually take?

Timing depends on the scope, complexity, and level of detail involved. Once I understand your brief and deliverables, I’ll outline a realistic schedule with clear review points before work begins.

Can you tailor a project to my needs?

Yes. Every collaboration is shaped around its goals, whether you’re developing a new product, refining a furniture concept, or exploring a one-off visual direction. We can define the scope together before deciding on the next steps.

Can I give feedback as the project develops?

Absolutely. We agree on review points at the outset, so there is time to discuss the work, resolve questions, and refine the chosen direction as it takes shape.

What tools do you use?

I use SolidWorks and Rhinoceros for CAD, Blender and Cycles for 3D modeling and rendering, and tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign for image refinement and presentation. I choose the workflow to suit each project’s needs and deliverables.
